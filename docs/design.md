# Design direction

The portfolio presents Tarun as a web developer and AI/ML enthusiast through concrete projects and a concise personal story. The visitor path is introduction → selected work → background → toolkit → experience → teaching → contact.

## References

- [Prince Keshri's LinkedIn post](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/princekeshri00_frontenddevelopment-javascript-css-ugcPost-7500926037581770752-74hq/) demonstrates a Mercedes F1-inspired site using Three.js and scroll-driven storytelling. Its influence here is a strong 3D introduction followed by deliberate section transitions. No source or assets were reused.
- [Apple](https://www.apple.com/) informs the spacious presentation, large typography, concise copy and a hero object given a clear visual stage. This is an independent design, not an Apple page or affiliation.

## Colour roles

| Colour | Role | Meaning in this portfolio |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Graphite `#0b0e11` | Hero and quiet dark surfaces | Focus; a clear stage for the sculpture and primary introduction. |
| Warm ivory `#f5f4f0` | Reading surfaces and primary dark-surface text | Clarity and comfortable, editorial reading. |
| Ink `#14191c` | Primary text | The strongest information hierarchy on light surfaces. |
| Muted slate `#606568` | Supporting text | Secondary explanations that remain legible. |
| Cyan `#8be9e4` | Build mode and web identity | Making usable interfaces and software. |
| Amber `#eac18a` | Maths mode, Axiom artwork and teaching | Reasoning, exactness and understanding. |
| Violet `#b2a0ff` | Explore mode and AI-learning identity | Exploration, learning and evaluation. |

Darker counterparts of accent colours are used for small labels on light backgrounds. Neutral tints distinguish surfaces; they do not indicate a separate skill level. No proficiency bars, invented scores or arbitrary achievement metrics are used.

## Original 3D and artwork

The hero is a locally generated chrome torus-knot sculpture with procedural studio lighting. Its three modes use different knot geometry and colour. Pointer movement gives a small change of view; it never captures scrolling. Thin orbits suggest connected ideas rather than a literal AI model.

Project panels use original CSS illustrations: a circle equation and answer choices for Axiom SAT, an isometric learning route for Forge, exact fractions for AxiomMath, and stacked records for PocketKV. They are visual summaries, not claims about those applications' exact interfaces.

Motion is optional and restrained. There is no forced loading sequence, autoplay video, scroll hijacking or custom cursor. Visitors can navigate, inspect cases and contact Tarun without interacting with the sculpture.
